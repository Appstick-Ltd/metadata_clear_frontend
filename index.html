Sanitization Modes

Choose How You Clean or Mask

Switch seamlessly between total metadata erasure and harmless cosmetic masking.

Wipe Mode

Total Erasure

Completely strips all EXIF, XMP, IPTC, GPS coordinates, camera serials, AI generation prompts, and document author properties, leaving tags blank.

  • Removes APP1/APP13, PNG tEXt/iTXt/zTXt, and WEBP EXIF/XMP chunks
  • Blanks PDF /Info dictionaries and DOCX docProps XML fields
  • Ideal for social sharing, AI art publishing, and confidential PDFs

Ghost Masking

Cosmetic Spoof

Replaces real identifying metadata with normal, generic professional values instead of leaving empty fields where parsers expect metadata headers.

  • Substitutes personal author, device, and software tags safely
  • Prevents structural distortion in strict enterprise workflows
Transparency Note: Cosmetic metadata replacement only — not verifiable cryptographic provenance.

FAQ

Quick Answers

Everything you need to know about how Metadata Cleaner protects your files.

Does Metadata Cleaner upload my photos or documents to any server?
No. All scanning, AI prompt detection, and sanitization run 100% locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded or logged remotely.
Does cleaning an image reduce its resolution or visual quality?
No. In Wipe and Ghost Mask modes, Metadata Cleaner strips or replaces metadata markers and binary chunks directly without re-compressing image pixels.
Which AI image metadata tags can Metadata Cleaner detect and strip?
Metadata Cleaner detects embedded prompts, negative prompts, seeds, parameters, ComfyUI workflow JSONs, Midjourney/DALL-E/ChatGPT/Firefly/Imagen/NovelAI/InvokeAI tags, and C2PA / digitalSourceType indicators inside PNG, JPG, and WEBP files.
What is the difference between Wipe Mode and Ghost Masking?
Wipe Mode erases all sensitive metadata leaving fields blank or removed. Ghost Masking replaces personal tags with generic, realistic cosmetic placeholders when you don’t want empty headers.
