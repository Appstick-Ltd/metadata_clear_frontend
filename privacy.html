Legal & Privacy

Metadata Cleaner Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Version 1.0 • App ID: bd.com.appstick.metadatacleaner • Developer: Appstick Ltd

On-Device File Processing Guarantee: All file inspection, AI prompt detection, and metadata sanitization happen strictly inside your device sandbox. Your photos, PDFs, and DOCX files are never uploaded to any server.
1

Local Processing & Zero File Retention

Metadata Cleaner processes your images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) and documents (PDF, DOCX) strictly locally on your device. We have zero access to the contents or metadata of files you inspect or sanitize.

  • Zero file uploads to external or cloud servers.
  • Temporary in-memory buffers are cleared immediately upon task completion.
  • No remote logging, telemetry, or audit trails of your files.
2

Account & Authentication Information

Metadata Cleaner offers optional or user-initiated account sign-in via Firebase Authentication (Email/Password and Google Sign-In). When you sign in, only the minimal authentication details required to manage your account are processed:

  • Collected Account Data: Name, email address, and Firebase Authentication User ID (UID).
  • Purpose: Account authentication, profile display, and session management.
  • Security: All authentication traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties or advertisers.
3

Metadata Stripping & Sanitization Modes

Metadata Cleaner provides two local sanitization modes so you remain in full control of your files:

  • Wipe Mode: Completely strips EXIF, XMP, IPTC, GPS coordinates, camera serials, author properties, and embedded AI generation prompts/workflows.
  • Ghost Mask Mode: Replaces identifying metadata headers with harmless generic/synthetic placeholders. Transparency Note: Ghost Masking performs cosmetic replacement only and does not provide verifiable cryptographic provenance.
4

Device Permissions Transparency

We follow strict least-privilege permissions. Metadata Cleaner only accesses a file or camera capture when you explicitly select files via the system picker or tap the camera option inside the app.

  • No broad storage scanning or background directory access.
  • No access to contacts, call logs, SMS, or background location history.
  • Clean copies are saved safely while respecting your Keep Original File backup preference.
5

No Ads & Zero Behavioral Trackers

Metadata Cleaner contains zero third-party advertising networks and zero behavioral analytics or ad-tracking SDKs. Your usage of the app is never profiled for marketing.

6

Data Retention, Ownership & Account Deletion

You retain 100% legal ownership and copyright over all documents and images processed with Metadata Cleaner. Sanitization history and preferences are stored locally on your device and cleared automatically if you delete your account or uninstall the app.

You can permanently delete your account and associated authentication data anytime inside the app (Settings → Delete Account) or via our Account Deletion page.

7
