Privacy Policy

Metadata Cleaner is built from the ground up to protect your personal privacy. All file inspection and metadata sanitization occur strictly inside your device.

Effective Date: November 2026 Version: 1.0.0 Package: bd.com.appstick.metadatacleaner Developer: Appstick Ltd
100% On-Device Processing Guarantee: Metadata Cleaner never uploads your photos, PDFs, or DOCX documents to any external or cloud server. Temporary memory buffers are wiped immediately upon task completion.

1 Local Processing Guarantee

Metadata Cleaner processes your images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) and documents (PDF, DOCX) strictly on your mobile device. All metadata inspection, AI prompt detection, and byte sanitization algorithms run entirely offline within your device's sandbox.

  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Your files are never transmitted to Appstick servers or third parties.
  • Immediate Buffer Clearance: In-memory file bytes are cleared from RAM right after cleaning.
  • No Remote Telemetry: We do not log filenames, file contents, hashes, or locations.

2 Data We Do Not Collect

To maintain the highest privacy standards, Metadata Cleaner strictly refrains from collecting any of the following:

  • Your photos, videos, documents, or their embedded contents.
  • Original or cleaned metadata (EXIF tags, GPS coordinates, author names, AI generation prompts).
  • Device location history, IP location logging, or network traffic dumps.
  • Personal contacts, SMS, call history, or other sensitive smartphone data.

3 Optional Account & Authentication

Metadata Cleaner provides optional cloud profile syncing via Firebase Authentication (Email/Password and Google Sign-In). Account creation is not required to use the core offline metadata cleaning tools.

If you voluntarily choose to sign in, we process only the minimal authentication records required by Firebase:

  • Collected Data: Your email address, display name, and unique Firebase Auth User ID (UID).
  • Purpose: Authentication session state, profile synchronization, and access management.
  • Security: All communication with Firebase Authentication is strictly encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3. We never sell, rent, or trade your credentials.

4 Sanitization Modes & Processing

Metadata Cleaner offers two distinct local sanitization modes:

  • Wipe Mode: Completely deletes EXIF, XMP, IPTC, GPS coordinates, camera serials, and AI generation prompts from binary file headers.
  • Ghost Mask Mode: Replaces identifying tags with realistic, generic placeholders (e.g. standard camera models) so parsers that expect metadata headers do not report syntax errors.

Transparency Note: Ghost Masking is intended solely for harmless cosmetic privacy replacement and does not constitute cryptographic provenance proof.

5 Device Permissions Transparency

Metadata Cleaner requests only the permissions strictly necessary to perform file sanitization:

  • Storage / Media Picker Access: Used exclusively to open files you specifically choose to inspect or clean, and to save sanitized copies in your output folder.
  • Camera (Optional): Used only if you choose the "Take Photo" feature inside the app to capture and immediately strip metadata from a fresh picture.
  • Network (Optional): Used solely for optional Firebase Authentication sign-in, account management, and checking for app updates. Core file sanitization works 100% offline without internet.

6 No Ads & Zero Third-Party Trackers

Metadata Cleaner contains zero third-party advertising SDKs (no Google AdMob, Unity, Meta Ads, etc.) and zero behavioral analytics trackers. We do not track your usage habits across applications or sell data to brokers.

7 Data Retention, Ownership & Account Deletion

You retain 100% ownership and intellectual property rights over all files you inspect and clean. Your file cleaning history is saved locally in your device database and is cleared immediately if you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

If you created an optional Firebase account, you can permanently delete it at any time:

  • In-App: Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • Online / Support: Follow the steps on our dedicated Account Deletion Page or email info@appstick.com.bd.

8 Children's Privacy

Metadata Cleaner is a general audience utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
