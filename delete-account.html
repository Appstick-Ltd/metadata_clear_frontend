Delete Account & Data

Learn how to easily and permanently remove your Metadata Cleaner account and associated information.

App: Metadata Cleaner Package: bd.com.appstick.metadatacleaner Developer: Appstick Ltd
Important Reminder About Your Files: Metadata Cleaner performs all metadata scrubbing 100% locally on your smartphone. We do not store your photos, PDFs, or DOCX files on our servers. Only optional Firebase Authentication records exist if you created an account.

1 Option A: Delete Instantly Inside the App (Recommended)

You can permanently erase your account and wipe all local application cache immediately from within the Metadata Cleaner mobile app:

  1. Open Metadata Cleaner on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Sign in to the account you wish to delete (if not already signed in).
  3. Tap the Settings icon or tab in the navigation bar.
  4. Under the ACCOUNT section, tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your decision in the prompt dialog.

Your Firebase Authentication user record, in-app sanitization history, preferences, and temporary output sandbox folders are immediately wiped.

2 Option B: Request Deletion via Email (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your mobile device, you can submit an account deletion request directly to our support team:

Request Deletion via Email Recipient: info@appstick.com.bd

Please make sure to send the request from the email address registered with your Metadata Cleaner account so we can verify account ownership.

3 What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication user account (email address, display name, user ID, linked Google OAuth tokens) and in-app sanitization logs.
  • Never Stored: Your files (JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF, DOCX), their original metadata, and stripped content never leave your device and are never held on Appstick servers.
  • Retained Records: Zero personal data is retained after deletion, except standard email records if you submitted an email inquiry.

4 Processing Timeline

  • In-App Deletion: Takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.
  • Email Requests: Processed within 7 business days following identity verification. A confirmation email will be sent once the account has been deleted.
