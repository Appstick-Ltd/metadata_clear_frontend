Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Metadata Cleaner Account

App: Metadata Cleaner (bd.com.appstick.metadatacleaner) • Developer: Appstick Ltd

Important Note About Your Files: Metadata Cleaner processes all photos, PDFs, and DOCX files 100% locally on your device. We never store your files on our servers — only your authentication account record (if you signed in) exists in Firebase Authentication.
1

Option A: Delete Instantly Inside the App (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your account and all local user data immediately from inside the Metadata Cleaner Android app:

  1. Open Metadata Cleaner on your device and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Under ACCOUNT, tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete in the confirmation sheet.

Your Firebase Authentication user record, local sanitization history, saved preferences, and temporary sandbox folder (ZeroTrace_Clean) are wiped immediately.

2

Option B: Request Deletion via Web / Email (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the app installed on your device, you can request permanent deletion of your Metadata Cleaner account by emailing our support team from the email address linked to your account:

Request Account Deletion by Email Send to: info@appstick.com.bd

For security, we verify ownership using your registered email address and permanently remove your Firebase Authentication account within 7 business days.

3

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted Immediately / Upon Verification: Firebase Authentication profile (email address, display name, auth UID, linked Google Sign-In provider record), plus in-app sanitization history, preferences, and local sandbox cache when deleted in-app.
  • Never Collected or Stored on Servers: Any JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF, or DOCX files you scanned or cleaned, along with their metadata — these never leave your device.
  • Retained Data: Zero personal data is retained after deletion is completed (except standard email correspondence logs if you contacted support, which can also be purged upon request).
